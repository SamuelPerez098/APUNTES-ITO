
# Reported Speech

El **Reported Speech (estilo indirecto)** se utiliza para comunicar lo que otra persona dijo **sin repetir exactamente sus palabras**.

### Direct Speech

> John said, **"I am tired."**

### Reported Speech

> John said **that he was tired**.

La idea principal es:

```
Direct Speech → Reported Speech
```

---

# 2. Estructura básica

### Statements

```
Subject + said (that) + Reported Statement
```

o:

```
Subject + told + Object + (that) + Reported Statement
```

### Examples

> She **said that** she was tired.

> She **told me that** she was tired.

> [!warning] SAY vs TELL  
> **say + something**
> 
> **tell + someone + something**
> 
> ❌ She told that she was tired.
> 
> ✅ She **said that** she was tired.
> 
> ✅ She **told me that** she was tired.

---

# 3. Cambio de tiempos verbales

Cuando el verbo introductorio está en pasado (`said`, `told`, etc.), normalmente hacemos **backshift**: el tiempo verbal retrocede un paso.

|Direct Speech|Reported Speech|
|---|---|
|Present Simple|**Past Simple**|
|Present Continuous|**Past Continuous**|
|Present Perfect|**Past Perfect**|
|Past Simple|**Past Perfect**|
|Past Continuous|**Past Perfect Continuous**|
|Past Perfect|**Past Perfect**|
|will|**would**|
|can|**could**|
|may|**might**|

---

# 4. Present Simple → Past Simple

### Direct

> She said, **"I work here."**

### Reported

> She said that she **worked** there.

```
Present Simple → Past Simple
```

---

# 5. Present Continuous → Past Continuous

### Direct

> He said, **"I am studying."**

### Reported

> He said that he **was studying**.

```
Present Continuous → Past Continuous
```

---

# 6. Present Perfect → Past Perfect

### Direct

> She said, **"I have finished."**

### Reported

> She said that she **had finished**.

```
Present Perfect → Past Perfect
```

---

# 7. Past Simple → Past Perfect

### Direct

> He said, **"I visited London."**

### Reported

> He said that he **had visited** London.

```
Past Simple → Past Perfect
```

---

# 8. Past Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous

### Direct

> She said, **"I was working."**

### Reported

> She said that she **had been working**.

```
Past Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous
```

---

# 9. Past Perfect

Normalmente **no cambia**.

### Direct

> He said, **"I had finished."**

### Reported

> He said that he **had finished**.

```
Past Perfect → Past Perfect
```

---

# 10. WILL → WOULD

### Direct

> She said, **"I will call you."**

### Reported

> She said that she **would call** me.

```
will → would
```

---

### Direct

> He said, **"I can swim."**

> He said that he **could swim**.

```
can → could
```

# 12. MAY → MIGHT

### Direct

> She said, **"I may go."**

# 13. Cambios de pronombres

Los pronombres deben cambiar dependiendo de **quién está hablando**.

### Direct

> John said, **"I love my car."**

### Reported

> John said that **he** loved **his** car.

```
I → he/she
my → his/her
we → they
our → their
```

No es un cambio mecánico: debes considerar **quién dijo la frase y a quién se refiere**.

---

# 14. Cambios de expresiones de tiempo y lugar

También pueden cambiar las expresiones relacionadas con tiempo y lugar.

|Direct|Reported|
|---|---|
|**now**|then|
|**today**|that day|
|**tomorrow**|the next day / the following day|
|**yesterday**|the day before / the previous day|
|**this**|that|
|**these**|those|
|**here**|there|
|**ago**|before|
|**last week**|the previous week|
|**next week**|the following week|

### Example

Direct:

> She said, **"I will see you tomorrow."**

Reported:

> She said that she would see me **the next day**.

---

# 15. Reported Questions

Cuando reportamos una pregunta, **ya no utilizamos el orden interrogativo**.

### Direct

> He asked, **"Where do you live?"**

### Reported

> He asked me **where I lived**.

Observa:

```
Direct:
Where + do + you + live?

Reported:
where + you + lived
```

> [!warning] Regla muy importante  
> En una **reported question** NO hacemos inversión sujeto-verbo.
> 
> ❌ He asked where **did I live**.
> 
> ✅ He asked where **I lived**.

---

# 16. WH-Questions

Con preguntas que ya tienen `who`, `what`, `where`, `when`, `why`, `how`, conservamos la palabra interrogativa.

### Structure

```
Subject + asked + Object + WH-word + Subject + Verb
```

### Example

Direct:

> She asked, **"Where do you work?"**

Reported:

> She asked me **where I worked**.

Direct:

> He asked, **"Why are you crying?"**

Reported:

> He asked me **why I was crying**.

---

# 17. Yes/No Questions

Cuando la pregunta puede responderse con **yes/no**, utilizamos principalmente:

> **if** o **whether**

### Structure

```
Subject + asked + Object + if/whether + Subject + Verb
```

### Direct

> She asked, **"Are you tired?"**

### Reported

> She asked me **if I was tired**.

También:

> She asked me **whether I was tired**.

---

# 18. Reported Commands

Para órdenes utilizamos:

```
Subject + told + Object + to + Verb
```

### Direct

> The teacher said, **"Open your books."**

### Reported

> The teacher **told us to open** our books.

---

# 19. Negative Commands

Para órdenes negativas:

```
Subject + told + Object + not to + Verb
```

### Direct

> He said, **"Don't touch it."**

### Reported

> He **told me not to touch** it.

---

# 20. Reported Requests

Para peticiones podemos utilizar:

```
ask + Object + to + Verb
```

### Direct

> She said, **"Please help me."**

### Reported

> She **asked me to help** her.

---

# 21. Say vs Tell vs Ask

Esta diferencia es muy importante.

|Verb|Structure|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**say**|say + something|She said that she was tired.|
|**tell**|tell + someone + something|She told me that she was tired.|
|**ask**|ask + someone + question|She asked me where I lived.|
|**ask**|ask + someone + to + Verb|She asked me to help.|

### Memoriza:

```
SAY + something
TELL + someone + something
ASK + someone + question
ASK + someone + TO + Verb
```

---

# 22. Reported Speech con Modal Verbs

Algunos modales cambian:

|Direct|Reported|
|---|---|
|can|**could**|
|may|**might**|
|will|**would**|
|shall|**should / would**|
|must|**had to** _(obligación)_|

### Example

> "I **can** swim."

→ She said she **could** swim.

> "I **will** help."

→ He said he **would** help.

> "I **must** leave."

→ She said she **had to** leave.

---

# 23. Modales que normalmente NO cambian

Estos normalmente permanecen igual:

```
could
would
should
might
ought to
```

Example:

> "I **should** study."

→ He said that he **should** study.

---

# 24. Cuándo NO es necesario hacer Backshift

El cambio de tiempo no siempre es obligatorio.

Si estamos hablando de una **verdad general o algo que sigue siendo cierto**, podemos mantener el tiempo original.

### Direct

> The teacher said, **"Water boils at 100°C."**

### Reported

> The teacher said that water **boils** at 100°C.

No necesitamos cambiar:

> boils → boiled

porque sigue siendo una verdad general.

---

# 25. Resumen para memorizar

### Statements

```
SAID + (THAT) + Subject + Verb
TOLD + Object + (THAT) + Subject + Verb
```

### Questions

```
ASKED + Object + WH-word + Subject + Verb
```

```
ASKED + Object + IF/WHETHER + Subject + Verb
```

### Commands

```
TOLD + Object + TO + Verb
```

### Negative Commands

```
TOLD + Object + NOT TO + Verb
```

### Requests

```
ASKED + Object + TO + Verb
```
