
> [!info] # Interrogative Questions

# 1. Short Questions

### Structure

```
Question Word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb + Complement?
```

### Use

- Obtener información específica
- Preguntar directamente sobre una persona, cosa, lugar, tiempo, razón o manera
- Utilizar palabras interrogativas como **who, what, where, when, why, how**

### Question Words

- who → quién
- what → qué / cuál
- where → dónde
- when → cuándo
- why → por qué
- which → cuál / cuáles
- whose → de quién
- how → cómo

### Examples

- What do you want?
- Where does she live?
- When did they arrive?
- Why are you studying English?
- How does this work?

### Important Rule

Cuando utilizamos un auxiliar (**do, does, did, will, have, has, had, etc.**), el verbo principal normalmente permanece en su **forma base** cuando corresponde.

- What **did** you **eat**?
- Where **does** he **work**?
- When **will** she **arrive**?

## 2. Long Questions

### Structure

```
Question Word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb + Complement?
```

Las preguntas largas utilizan la misma estructura básica que las preguntas cortas, pero contienen información adicional.

### Examples

- What did you do yesterday after work?
- Where does your brother usually study English?
- Why did she leave the university last year?
- When will they finish the project?
- How long have you been studying English?

### Important Rule

Aunque la pregunta sea larga, el orden de la pregunta **no cambia**:

```
Question Word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb
```

+ Why **did** she **leave** the university?

❌ Why she **did leave** the university?

### Question with a Preposition

Algunas preguntas pueden terminar con una preposición:

```
Question Word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb + Preposition?
```

- Who are you talking **to**?
- What are you looking **for**?
- Which university did he graduate **from**?

## 3. INDIRECT QUESTIONS

### Structure

```
Introductory Phrase + Question Word + Subject + Verb + Complement
```

### Use

- Hacer preguntas de manera más formal o cortés
- Utilizar preguntas dentro de otra oración
- Preguntar información indirectamente
- Aparecen frecuentemente en contextos académicos y formales

### Common Introductory Phrases

- Could you tell me...
- Can you tell me...
- Do you know...
- Would you mind telling me...
- I wonder...
- I'd like to know...
- Do you happen to know...

### Examples

Direct Question:

> Where does she live?

Indirect Question:

> Could you tell me **where she lives**?

Direct Question:

> What time does the class start?

Indirect Question:

> Do you know **what time the class starts**?

Direct Question:

> Why did he leave?

Indirect Question:

> Do you know **why he left**?

### Important Rule

> [!warning] NO INVERSION  
> En una **Indirect Question**, después de la palabra interrogativa utilizamos el orden de una **oración afirmativa**:
> 
> **Subject + Verb**
> 
> No utilizamos:
> 
> **Auxiliary + Subject + Verb**

### Direct vs Indirect

**Direct Question**

```
Where + does + she + live?
```

**Indirect Question**

```
Could you tell me + where + she + lives?
```

---

### Yes / No Indirect Questions

Cuando la pregunta directa **no tiene Question Word**, utilizamos:

**if / whether**

### Structure

```
Introductory Phrase + if/whether + Subject + Verb + Complement
```

### Examples

Direct:

> Is she coming?

Indirect:

> Do you know **if she is coming**?

Direct:

> Did they finish the project?

Indirect:

> Do you know **if they finished the project**?

Direct:

> Will he attend the meeting?

Indirect:

> Could you tell me **whether he will attend the meeting**?

## 4. WH- Questions vs IF/WHÉTHER

### WH- Questions

Utilizamos la misma palabra interrogativa:

```
where
when
why
what
who
which
how
```

Example:

> Where does he live?

→ Do you know **where he lives**?

### Yes / No Questions

Utilizamos:

```
if / whether
```

Example:

> Does he live here?

→ Do you know **if he lives here**?

---

## Quick Comparison

|Type|Structure|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Direct|WH + Aux + Subject + Verb|Where does she live?|
|Indirect WH|Intro + WH + Subject + Verb|Do you know where she lives?|
|Direct Yes/No|Aux + Subject + Verb|Does she live here?|
|Indirect Yes/No|Intro + if/whether + Subject + Verb|



> [!info] # IF vs WHETHER


### Use

**If** y **whether** pueden utilizarse para expresar una pregunta de **sí o no** de manera indirecta.

Ambos pueden significar: 
- si 
- si algo es cierto o no 
- si algo ocurrirá o no
-
### Structure

```
Introductory Phrase + if/whether + Subject + Verb + Complement
```


### Examples

Direct Question:

> Is she coming?

Indirect Question:

> Do you know **if she is coming**?
> 
> Do you know **whether she is coming**?

Direct Question:

> Did they finish the project?

Indirect Question:

> I don't know **if they finished the project**.
> 
> I don't know **whether they finished the project**.

## 1. IF

### Use

- Preguntas indirectas de **Yes/No**
- Situaciones donde se plantea una posibilidad
- Condiciones

### Examples

- Do you know **if he is coming**?
- I don't know **if she has finished**.
- Can you tell me **if they are ready**?

> [!warning] Importante  
> En una **Indirect Question**, `if` significa **"si"** y no debe confundirse con el `if` de las oraciones condicionales.

**Indirect Question:**

> I don't know **if he is coming**.

→ No sé **si viene**.

**Conditional:**

> **If he comes**, I will talk to him.

→ **Si viene**, hablaré con él.

## 2. WHETHER

### Use

- Preguntas indirectas de **Yes/No**
- Cuando existen dos o más posibilidades
- Situaciones donde significa **"si... o no"**
- Después de determinadas preposiciones
- Antes de un infinitivo (**whether to...**)
- En estructuras formales

### Examples

- I don't know **whether he is coming**.
- She asked **whether I was ready**.
- We need to decide **whether we should stay**.

## 3. WHETHER + OR NOT

### Structure

```
Whether + Subject + Verb + or not
```

### Examples

- I don't know **whether he will come or not**.
- She asked **whether we were ready or not**.
- We need to decide **whether to stay or not**.

También puede aparecer:

```
Whether or not + Subject + Verb
```

- **Whether or not he comes**, we will start.
- **Whether or not she agrees**, we will continue.

## 4. WHETHER + TO + VERB

`Whether` puede utilizarse antes de un infinitivo.

### Structure

```
Whether + to + Verb
```

### Examples

- I don't know **whether to go**.
- She can't decide **whether to stay**.
- We need to determine **whether to accept** the offer.

> [!warning] Importante  
> No utilizamos **if + to + verb** en esta estructura.
> 
> ❌ I don't know **if to go**.
> 
> ✅ I don't know **whether to go**.

## 5. Después de una preposición

Después de una preposición utilizamos **whether**, no **if**.

### Structure

```
Preposition + whether + Subject + Verb
```

### Examples

- It depends on **whether he agrees**.
- We talked about **whether we should leave**.
- It is unclear as to **whether they will accept**.

> [!warning] TOEFL  
> **Whether** es la opción correcta después de una preposición.
> 
> ❌ It depends on **if** he agrees.
> 
> ✅ It depends on **whether** he agrees.

## 6. IF vs WHETHER — CUANDO AMBOS SON POSIBLES

En muchas **Indirect Questions**, ambos son correctos:

- I don't know **if she is coming**.
- I don't know **whether she is coming**.
- Do you know **if he passed**?
- Do you know **whether he passed**?

En estos casos, **whether** suele sonar un poco más formal.

## Quick Comparison

|Situation|IF|WHETHER|
|---|---|---|
|Indirect Yes/No Question|✅|✅|
|"Si algo ocurre o no"|✅|✅|
|Whether/if + Subject + Verb|✅|✅|
|Whether + or not|❌*|✅|
|Whether + to + Verb|❌|✅|
|Después de preposición|❌|✅|
|Estructura formal|✅|✅|
|Condicional|✅|❌|
