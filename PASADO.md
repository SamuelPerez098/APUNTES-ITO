
> [!info]  # Past Simple

### Structure

```
Subject + Verb (past form) + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones dadas por terminadas en el pasado
- Acciones que ocurrieron en un momento específico del pasado
- Secuencia de acciones,hechos o historias en el pasado
- Hábitos o situaciones que existían en el pasado

### Signal words

- yesterday
- last night
- last week
- last month
- last year
- ago
- in 202X / 19XX
- when I was young
- once
- then

### Examples 

- I played soccer yesterday.
- She studied English last night.
- They visited Mexico last year.

### Negative

```
Subject + did + not + Verb (base form) + Complement
```


### Questions

```
Did + Subject + Verb (base form) + Complement?
```




> [!info] # Past Continuous

### Structure

```
Subject + was/were + Verb-ing + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que estaban ocurriendo en un momento específico del pasado
- Acciones en progreso que fueron interrumpidas o incompletas por otra acción
- Dos acciones que estaban ocurriendo simultáneamente
- Describir el contexto o situación en un momento del pasado

### Signal words

- while
- when
- as
- at that moment
- at that time
- all day
- all night
- yesterday at 8:00
- this time yesterday

### Examples 

- I was studying English at 8:00.
- She was cooking when I arrived.
- They were watching a movie while we were studying.

### Negative

```
Subject + was/were + not + Verb-ing + Complement
```


### Questions

```
Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing + Complement?
```



> [!info] # Past Perfect

### Structure

```
Subject + had + Past Participle + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que ocurrieron antes de otra acción en el pasado (antes de algo en el pasado)
- Para establecer cuál de dos acciones ocurrió primero
- Acciones que ya habían terminado antes de un momento específico del pasado
- Para explicar la causa o el contexto de una situación pasada

### Signal words

- before
- after
- already
- just
- never
- ever
- by the time
- until then
- when
- once
- as soon as

### Examples 

- I had finished my homework before I went to bed.
- She had already left when I arrived.
- They had never seen snow before they moved there.
- By the time we arrived, the movie had started.

### Negative

```
Subject + had + not + Past Participle + Complement
```


### Questions

```
Had + Subject + Past Participle + Complement?
```


> [!info] # Past Perfect Continuous


### Structure

```
Subject + had + been + Verb-ing + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que habían estado ocurriendo durante un período de tiempo antes de otra acción pasada
- Enfatizar la duración de una acción antes de un momento específico del pasado
- Acciones que continuaron hasta que otra acción ocurrió
- Explicar la causa o evidencia de una situación pasada

### Signal words

- for
- since
- before
- until
- until then
- by the time
- how long
- all day
- all morning
- all night
- before
- when

### Examples

- I had been studying for three hours before I went to bed.
- She had been working there since 2015 before she moved to another city.
- They had been playing soccer all afternoon when it started to rain.
- He was tired because he had been working all day.

### Negative

```
Subject + had + not + been + Verb-ing + Complement
```


### Questions

```
`Had + Subject + been + Verb-ing + Complement?`
```
