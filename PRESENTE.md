
> [!info]  # Present Simple

### Structure

```
text Subject + Verb (base form) + Complement 
```

### Use

- Habitos
- Routinas
- Hechos
- Estados o relaciones que no cambian 

### Signal words

- always 
- usually 
- often 
- sometimes 
- never 
- every day

### Examples 

- I play soccer.
- She studies English.

### Negative

```
text Subject + do/does + not + Verb + Complement
```


### Questions

```
Do/Does + Subject + Verb + Complement?
```


> [!info] # Present Continuos 

### Structure

```
Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que están ocurriendo en este momento
- Acciones temporales
- Situaciones que están cambiando o desarrollándose
- Planes futuros ya organizados

### Signal words

- now
- right now
- at the moment
- currently
- today
- this week
- this month
- these days

### Examples

- I am studying English.
- She is working right now.
- They are watching a movie.
- We are meeting tomorrow.

### Negative

```
Subject + am/is/are + not + Verb-ing + Complement
```

### Questions

```
Am/Is/Are + Subject + Verb-ing + Complement?
```



> [!info] # Present Perfect

### Structure

```
Subject + have/has +  Verb. Past Participle + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que ocurrieron en el pasado pero tienen una conexión con el presente
- Experiencias en algún momento de la vida
- Acciones que comenzaron en el pasado y continúan hasta el presente
- Acciones que ocurrieron recientemente, Noticias o expresiones de tiempo 
- Acciones pasadas cuando no se especifica cuándo ocurrieron

### Signal words

- ever
- never
- already
- yet
- just
- recently
- lately
- so far
- until now
- up to now
- since
- for
- before
- once
- several times

### Examples

- I have visited Mexico.
- She has already finished her homework.
- They have lived here for five years.
- He has never seen that movie.

### Negative

```
Subject + have/has + not + Past Participle + Complement
```

### Questions

```
Have/Has + Subject + Past Participle + Complement?
```

> [!tip] # Yet, Still & Already ### YET

Tanto YET, STILL , ALREADY significan lo mismo en ingles 

# YET

significa aun , todavia , ya? , entre oraciones( pero siempre va al final)
**Yet** normalmente aparece al final de la oración.

### USE

- Se utiliza principalmente en **preguntas** y **oraciones negativas**.
- Indica que algo **no ha ocurrido hasta el momento presente**.
- Normalmente se coloca **al final de la oración**.
### Structure

```
Have/Has + Subject + Past Participle + ... + yet?
```

### Examples

- Have you finished your homework **yet**?
- She hasn't arrived **yet**.
- They haven't eaten **yet**.

# ALREADY

**Use**

- Indica que una acción **ya ocurrió**.
- Generalmente expresa que algo ocurrió **antes de lo esperado**.
- Se utiliza principalmente en **oraciones afirmativas**.
- También puede utilizarse en preguntas.

### Structure

```
Subject + have/has + already + Past Participle + Complement
```

### Examples

- I have **already finished** my homework.
- She has **already left**.
- They have **already eaten**.

### Questions

```
Have/Has + Subject + already + Past Participle + ...?
```

### Examples

- Have you **already finished**?
- Has she **already left**?

### Quick Comparison

|Word|Main idea|Typical position|
|---|---|---|
|**yet**|todavía / aún|Final|
|**still**|todavía / sigue|Before have/has|
|**already**|ya|After have/has|


> [!info] # Present Perfect Continuous

### Structure

```
Subject + have/has + been + Verb-ing + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que comenzaron en el pasado y continúan en el presente
- Acciones que han estado ocurriendo durante un período de tiempo
- Acciones que terminaron recientemente pero tienen un resultado o evidencia en el presente
- Enfatizar la duración de una acción

### Signal words

- for
- since
- all day
- all morning
- all week
- lately
- recently
- how long
- since then
- up to now

### Examples 

- I have been studying English for three hours.
- She has been working here since 2020.
- They have been playing soccer all afternoon.
- He has been studying a lot lately.

### Negative

```
Subject + have/has + not + been + Verb-ing + Complement
```


### Questions

```
`Have/Has + Subject + been + Verb-ing + Complement?`
```


El continuo expresa una actividad temporal , mientas el simple un estado permanente
