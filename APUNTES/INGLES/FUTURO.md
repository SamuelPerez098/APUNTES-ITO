
> [!info] # Future — Will & Be Going To

# 1. Future with WILL


### Structure

```
Subject + will + Verb (base form) + Complement
```

### Use

- Decisiones tomadas en el momento de hablar
- Predicciones basadas en opiniones, creencias o suposiciones
- Promesas
- Ofrecimientos
- Peticiones
- Amenazas
- Advertencias
- Eventos futuros que se consideran ciertos o inevitables

### Signal words

- tomorrow
- next week
- next month
- next year
- soon
- later
- someday
- I think
- I believe
- probably
- perhaps
- maybe
- I hope

### Examples

- I think it will rain tomorrow.
- She will probably pass the exam.
- I will help you.
- I promise I will study harder.
- Don't worry. I will call you later.

### Decision made at the moment

> [!info] Regla importante  
> Usamos **will** cuando la decisión se toma **en el momento de hablar**.

- A: The phone is ringing.
- B: I **will answer** it.

La decisión se tomó en ese momento.

### Prediction based on opinion

- I think she **will pass** the exam.
- I believe they **will win**.
- It **will probably rain** tomorrow.

### Negative

```
Subject + will not + Verb (base form) + Complement
```

**will not = won't**

- I will not go.
- I won't go.

### Questions

```
Will + Subject + Verb (base form) + Complement?
```

- Will you help me?
- Will she come tomorrow?
- Will they pass the exam?

## 2. Future with BE GOING TO

### Structure

```
Subject + am/is/are + going to + Verb (base form) + Complement
```

### Use

- Planes o intenciones que ya existen antes de hablar
- Decisiones que ya fueron tomadas
- Predicciones basadas en evidencia presente
- Acciones que están a punto de ocurrir debido a una situación actual

### Signal words

- tomorrow
- tonight
- next week
- next month
- next year
- soon
- this weekend
- later

### Examples

- I am going to study tonight.
- She is going to buy a new computer.
- They are going to travel next week.

### Plans already decided

> [!info] Regla importante  
> Usamos **be going to** cuando el plan o intención **ya existía antes del momento de hablar**.

- I am going to study tonight.

La decisión de estudiar ya estaba tomada.

### Prediction based on present evidence

> [!info] Regla importante  
> Usamos **be going to** cuando existe **evidencia presente** que indica que algo va a ocurrir.

- Look at those clouds. It is going to rain.
- Be careful! You are going to fall.
- That car is going too fast. It is going to crash.

### Negative

```
Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + Verb (base form) + Complement
```

- I am not going to go.
- She isn't going to study.
- They aren't going to travel.

### Questions

```
Am/Is/Are + Subject + going to + Verb (base form) + Complement?
```

- Are you going to study tonight?
- Is she going to travel?
- Are they going to buy a car?

## 3. WILL vs BE GOING TO

### Decision

**WILL** → decisión tomada ahora

**BE GOING TO** → decisión tomada anteriormente

- The phone is ringing. I **will answer** it.
- I **am going to buy** a new phone. I have already decided.

### Prediction

**WILL** → predicción basada en opinión o creencia

**BE GOING TO** → predicción basada en evidencia presente

- I think it **will rain** tomorrow.
- Look at those clouds! It **is going to rain**.

### Quick Comparison

|Situation|Future form|
|---|---|
|Decision made now|**will**|
|Previous plan/intention|**be going to**|
|Personal prediction/opinion|**will**|
|Prediction based on evidence|**be going to**|
|Promise|**will**|
|Offer|**will**|
|Warning based on evidence|**be going to**|


> [!info] # Future Continuous


### Structure

```
Subject + will be + Verb-ing + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que estarán ocurriendo en un momento específico del futuro
- Acciones que estarán en progreso durante un período determinado del futuro
- Describir una acción que estará ocurriendo cuando suceda otra acción futura
- Preguntar de manera más cortés sobre los planes de alguien

### Signal words

- this time tomorrow
- this time next week
- at this time
- tomorrow at 8:00
- next week at this time
- all day tomorrow
- all morning
- when
- while

### Examples

- I will be studying English at 8:00 tomorrow.
- She will be working at this time next week.
- They will be traveling when you arrive.
- This time tomorrow, we will be taking the exam.

### Negative

```
Subject + will not be + Verb-ing + Complement
```

**will not = won't**

- I won't be studying tonight.
- She won't be working tomorrow.

### Questions

```
Will + Subject + be + Verb-ing + Complement?
```

- Will you be studying tonight?
- Will she be working tomorrow?
- Will they be traveling next week?



> [!info] # Future Perfect

### Structure

```
Subject + will have + Past Participle + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que habrán terminado antes de un momento específico del futuro
- Acciones que estarán completadas antes de otra acción futura
- Expresar que algo estará terminado para una fecha o momento determinado
- Hablar de una acción futura desde la perspectiva de un punto posterior en el tiempo

### Signal words

- by
- by then
- by the time
- before
- by tomorrow
- by next week
- by next month
- by next year
- by the end of
- in + período de tiempo

### Examples

- I will have finished my homework by tomorrow.
- She will have graduated by next year.
- They will have arrived by the time we get there.
- By the end of the month, I will have completed the course.

### Negative

```
Subject + will not have + Past Participle + Complement
```

**will not = won't**

- I won't have finished the work by tomorrow.
- She won't have arrived by then.

### Questions

```
Will + Subject + have + Past Participle + Complement?
```

- Will you have finished the project by Friday?
- Will she have arrived by then?
- Will they have completed the course by next month?


> [!info] # Future Perfect Continuous

### Structure

```
Subject + will have been + Verb-ing + Complement
```

### Use

- Acciones que habrán estado ocurriendo durante un período de tiempo hasta un momento específico del futuro
- Enfatizar la duración de una acción hasta un momento futuro
- Acciones que comenzaron antes y continuarán hasta un momento determinado del futuro
- Expresar cuánto tiempo habrá durado una acción en el futuro

### Signal words

- for
- since
- by
- by then
- by the time
- by next year
- by next month
- by the end of
- for + period of time
- how long

### Examples

- By next year, I will have been studying English for three years.
- She will have been working here for ten years by June.
- By the time you arrive, I will have been waiting for two hours.
- By the end of the month, they will have been living here for five years.

### Negative

```
Subject + will not have been + Verb-ing + Complement
```

**will not = won't**

- I won't have been studying for long.
- She won't have been working here for ten years by June.

### Questions

```
Will + Subject + have been + Verb-ing + Complement?
```

- Will you have been studying English for three years by next year?
- Will she have been working here for ten years by June?
- Will they have been living there for five years by then?
