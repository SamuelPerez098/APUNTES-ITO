# EJEMPLOS 

### IN

1. Meses: in August, in March, in September
    
2. Años / Decenios / Siglos: in 2026, in the 1990s, in the 19th century
    
3. Estaciones del año: in autumn, in fall, in winter
    
4. Partes del día: in the early morning, in the late afternoon, in the evening
    
5. Lapsos o tiempo futuro (en / dentro de): in three days, in half an hour, in a year
    
6. Países, ciudades, continentes: in Canada, in Tokio, in Asia
    
7. Espacios cerrados o delimitados: in the kitchen, in the office, in a library, in the garden
    
8. Líquidos o masas (dentro de): in the ocean, in the soup, in the milk
    
9. Prensa o publicaciones escritas: in a journal, in a textbook, in the document
    

### ON

1. Días de la semana: in Tuesday, on Saturdays, on Thursdays
    
2. Fechas completas: on October 12th, on April 1st, 1999
    
3. Días especiales o festivos específicos: on my graduation day, on Thanksgiving, on Halloween
    
4. Partes de un día específico: on Monday afternoon, on Saturday evening
    
5. Superficies: on the desk, on the shelf, on the page, on the sidewalk
    
6. Nombres de calles y avenidas (sin número): on Main Street, on Broadway, on Sunset Boulevard
    
7. Medios de transporte públicos o grandes: on a subway train, on a ferry, on a cruise
    
8. Pisos de un edificio: on the ground floor, on the tenth floor, on the basement level
    
9. Medios de comunicación / pantallas: on the radio, on social media, on the screen
    

### AT

1. Horas específicas: at 7:15 AM, at 11:00, at midnight
    
2. Momentos concretos del día: at night, at daybreak, at dusk
    
3. Festividades o periodos (sin la palabra "Day"): at Thanksgiving, at the holidays
    
4. Expresiones fijas de tiempo: at present, at this stage, at the moment
    
5. Direcciones exactas (calle + número): at 221B Baker Street, at 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue
    
6. Puntos de referencia o ubicaciones específicas: at the corner, at the traffic light, at the counter
    
7. Lugares de actividad / Instituciones: at college, at the station, at the museum, at home
    
8. Eventos o reuniones: at a meeting, at a wedding, at a game




> [!info] # Adjectives


## 1. Descriptive Adjectives

### Use

Los adjetivos calificativos describen características, cualidades o estados de una persona, animal, objeto o situación.

### Examples

- a **big** house
- a **beautiful** city
- a **cold** day
- an **interesting** book

### Position

Normalmente aparecen: **Before a noun**

```
Adjective + Noun
```

- a **beautiful** woman
- an **expensive** car

**After a linking verb**

```
Subject + Linking Verb + Adjective
```

- She is **happy**.
- The exam was **difficult**.
- They seem **tired**.

## 2. Adjectives ending in -ING and -ED

### -ING

Los adjetivos terminados en **-ing** describen la cosa, persona o situación que **produce** una emoción o sensación.

### Examples

- The movie is **boring**.
- The class is **interesting**.
- The exam is **confusing**.
- The lesson is **exciting**.

### -ED

Los adjetivos terminados en **-ed** describen cómo **se siente una persona** como resultado de algo.

### Examples

- I am **bored**.
- She is **interested**.
- They are **confused**.
- He is **excited**.

### Quick Comparison

|-ING|-ED|
|---|---|
|Produces the feeling|Experiences the feeling|
|The movie is **boring**.|I am **bored**.|
|The class is **interesting**.|I am **interested**.|
|The exam is **confusing**.|She is **confused**.|
|The game is **exciting**.|They are **excited**.|

> [!warning] Regla importante  
> **-ING = causa de la emoción**
> 
> **-ED = persona que experimenta la emoción**

❌ I am boring.  
→ Significa que **soy aburrido / causo aburrimiento**.

✅ I am bored.  
→ Estoy aburrido.

## 3. Demonstrative Adjectives

### Use

Se utilizan para señalar o identificar un sustantivo según su **distancia** y **número**.

### Forms

||Singular|Plural|
|---|---|---|
|Near|**this**|**these**|
|Far|**that**|**those**|

### Structure

```
Demonstrative + Noun
```

### Examples

**This** → singular + cerca

- **This book** is interesting.

**These** → plural + cerca

- **These books** are interesting.

**That** → singular + lejos

- **That car** is expensive.

**Those** → plural + lejos

- **Those cars** are expensive.

> [!warning] Importante  
> Los demostrativos deben concordar con el sustantivo:
> 
> **this / that → singular**
> 
> **these / those → plural**

## 4. Possessive Adjectives

### Use

Indican quién posee o está relacionado con un sustantivo.

### Forms

|Subject Pronoun|Possessive Adjective|
|---|---|
|I|**my**|
|you|**your**|
|he|**his**|
|she|**her**|
|it|**its**|
|we|**our**|
|they|**their**|

### Structure

```
Possessive Adjective + Noun
```

### Examples

- **My car** is new.
- **Your house** is beautiful.
- **His brother** is a doctor.
- **Her phone** is expensive.
- **Its color** is unusual.
- **Our teacher** is helpful.
- **Their children** are at school.

> [!warning] Importante  
> Los possessive adjectives siempre necesitan un sustantivo después:
> 
> ✅ This is **my book**.
> 
> ❌ This is **my**.



> [!info] # Nouns

## 1. Countable Nouns

### Use

Son sustantivos que pueden contarse individualmente. Pueden utilizarse en:

- singular 
- plural 
- números

### Examples

- one **book** 
- two **books** 
- three **cars** 
- five **students**

### Singular

Los sustantivos contables singulares normalmente necesitan un determinante:


```
a/an + Singular Countable Noun
```

- a book
- a car
- an apple
- an exam

## 2. Uncountable Nouns

### Use

Son sustantivos que normalmente no se consideran unidades individuales y no se cuentan directamente.

Generalmente:

- No tienen plural
- No utilizan **a/an**
- Se utilizan con verbos en singular

### Common Examples

- water
- money
- information
- advice
- furniture
- equipment
- knowledge
- traffic
- news
- homework
- research
- progress

### Examples

- I need some **information**.
- She gave me some **advice**.
- The **furniture** is expensive.
- The **equipment** is new.
- The **information** was useful.

> [!warning] Errores comunes
> 
> ❌ an information
> 
> ✅ some information
> 
> ❌ advices
> 
> ✅ advice
> 
> ❌ furnitures
> 
> ✅ furniture
> 
> ❌ equipments
> 
> ✅ equipment

## 3. Countable vs Uncountable

|Countable|Uncountable|
|---|---|
|book|information|
|car|advice|
|student|furniture|
|apple|water|
|idea|knowledge|
|chair|equipment|
|job|homework|

### Countable

> I have **three books**.

### Uncountable

> I have **some information**.

## 4. Noun Phrases

### Use

Una **Noun Phrase** es un grupo de palabras cuyo elemento principal es un sustantivo.

Puede incluir:

- Determiners
- Adjectives
- Possessive adjectives
- Numbers
- Other nouns
- Prepositional phrases

### Basic Structure

```
Determiner + Adjective + Noun
```

### Examples

- **the big house**
- **a beautiful city**
- **an interesting book**
- **my new computer**

### With Multiple Adjectives

```
Determiner + Adjective + Adjective + Noun
```

- **a beautiful old house**
- **an expensive new car**
- **the large red building**

### With Numbers

```
Determiner/Number + Adjective + Noun
```

- **three large boxes**
- **two expensive cars**
- **the first important exam**

### With Prepositional Phrases

```
Determiner + Adjective + Noun + Prepositional Phrase
```

- **the big house on the hill**
- **a new computer for my brother**
- **the students in the classroom**

### With Multiple Nouns

```
Noun + Noun
```

El primer sustantivo funciona como modificador del segundo.

- **coffee shop**
- **school bus**
- **computer science**
- **English teacher**

### Complete Noun Phrase

```
Determiner + Adjective + Noun + Prepositional Phrase
```

- **The intelligent student from Mexico**
- **A large house near the university**
- **My new English teacher at school**

> [!warning] Identifying the Head Noun  
> En una Noun Phrase, el sustantivo principal se llama **Head Noun**.
> 
> **The large house on the hill**
> 
> Head Noun → **house**
> 
> **The intelligent students in the classroom**
> 
> Head Noun → **students**
